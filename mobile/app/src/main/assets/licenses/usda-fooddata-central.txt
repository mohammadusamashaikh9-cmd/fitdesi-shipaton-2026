USDA FoodData Central Foundation Foods
April 2026 release

Source: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service
FoodData Central: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
Dataset downloads: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
Foundation Foods documentation:
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/Foundation_Foods_Documentation/

FoodData Central data are United States public-domain data and are made
available under CC0 1.0 Universal. Permission is not required. Source citation
is requested; it is not a licence requirement.

FitDesi's public-safe runtime nutrition catalogue contains 39 verified USDA
FoodData Central records across four source classes: Foundation Foods (April
2026), Survey/FNDDS (2021-2023, October 2024), SR Legacy (April 2018 final
release), and Branded manufacturer-label-derived (April 2026). FitDesi selected
a bounded subset, retained immutable FDC IDs and exact per-100 g source values
in provenance/review metadata, mapped exact USDA nutrient identities, and
rounded displayed calories to the nearest whole kcal for the existing Android
catalogue schema. The April 2026 raw Foundation CSV dump is not redistributed in
the release branch; maintainers reacquire official source data from the USDA
download page above and verify the hash-pinned source archives, selection
evidence, and per-record provenance hashes stored with each verified record.
FitDesi does not imply USDA endorsement or professional nutrition certification.
